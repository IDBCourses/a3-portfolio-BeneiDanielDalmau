Cardboard Controller

Can cardboard curve?

The aim of this project was to build an approximate replica of a game controller out of easily available recycled cardboard material, using compositing of pieces and iterative work. My focus was on nailing the approximate scale of the reference controller while experimenting with the material to see how close I can get to replicating the original complex curves of the object. I limited my approach to strictly hand cut and measured parts.
