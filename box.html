Arduino Creature

Magnetic Container

An octagonal MDF box with embedded magnets for flexible mountability on metal surfaces. The same came from an approximation of a circle. It was modelled in 3d then the faces laid digitally on one plane for the laser cutting. The tiny magnets were glued into the inner wall of the box to hide them from the outside. It was a project to test laser cutting skills and is intended as a universal container for sensors or electronics.
